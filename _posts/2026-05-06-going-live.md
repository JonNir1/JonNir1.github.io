This website is live. That felt worth writing about.

I'm a researcher, not a web developer. I know my way around Python and a terminal, but CSS, Jekyll, and GitHub Pages are not exactly my natural habitat.
Building a personal academic website was always on the long to-do list, right below "update CV" and above "actually sleep eight hours."

What finally got it done was a combination of the [al-folio](https://github.com/alshedivat/al-folio) template and an AI coding agent (specifically, [Claude Code](https://claude.ai/code)). The workflow surprised me. Instead of googling my way through Stack Overflow threads, I described what I wanted in plain language and iterated. The agent read my CV, populated the YAML config files, fixed the git history, and wrote this very post's template - while I reviewed, corrected, and made the final calls.

It's a different way of working. Less "how do I do this" and more "here's what I want, let's figure out if that's the right thing to want." The cognitive load shifts from syntax to intent. For someone whose day job is asking questions about human cognition, that felt oddly fitting.

Thanks for visiting.
