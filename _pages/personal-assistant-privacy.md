**personal-assistant** is a private, single-user automation tool. It uses the Gmail API (`gmail.send` scope only) to send emails from the owner's own account to the owner and designated recipients. It does not read, collect, store, or share any Gmail data, and it serves no other users.

Contact me ([Jonathan Nir](mailto:jonathan.nir@mail.huji.ac.il)) for more detail.
