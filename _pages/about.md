I am a PhD candidate in the [ELSC Direct Track](https://elsc.huji.ac.il/) at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem, working in the [Deouell Lab](https://www.hcnl.org/) under the supervision of [Prof. Leon Deouell](https://en.huji.ac.il/en/leon-deouell). My research focuses on visual attention in naturalistic conditions, combining eye-tracking and EEG to study how humans allocate attention and process visual information in complex environments.

Before joining the PhD program, I worked as a software engineer at [Express Wi-Fi (Facebook / Meta)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Express_Wi-Fi) in Tel Aviv, where I led cross-functional projects spanning infrastructure, experimentation, and product.
I hold a BSc in Computer Science & Psychology from Tel Aviv University, with a research emphasis in neuroscience.

My work sits at the intersection of cognitive neuroscience and software engineering. I develop and maintain [pEYES](https://github.com/JonNir1/pEYES), an open-source Python toolkit for eye-tracking data analysis and algorithm evaluation. I'm proficient in Python, data science, and multimodal physiological data analysis (EEG + eye-tracking).

On a personal note — I share my life with my wife Hadas, our daughter Naama 👧, our dog Honey 🐶, and our cat Meow 🐱.
I like hiking but don't get to do it as much as I'd like. I also enjoy cooking, weightlifting and yoga.
